PRIVACY

This game stores certain game information locally in your browser using browser storage. This may include campaign progress, high scores, settings, unlocked items, mission progress, and similar game-state information.

This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to Nodeflow unless a future feature expressly states otherwise. Clearing your browser data may delete this information.

The Website is delivered using Cloudflare services. When you access the Website, Cloudflare may process technical information associated with requests to the Website, such as IP addresses, request information, browser or device information, timestamps, and security-related information. This processing may be necessary to deliver content, maintain performance, detect abuse, and protect the Website from malicious traffic. Cloudflare processes certain customer log information as a processor on behalf of its customers and may act as a controller for certain information in other circumstances. For more information, please review Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
